About Me

Get to know the person behind the code

Who Am I?

My name is Yandisa Mbentsula, and I come from the beautiful town of Centane in the Eastern Cape, South Africa. Growing up in a close-knit community taught me the value of hard work, respect, and perseverance.

I am currently a student at Walter Sisulu University, pursuing a qualification in Application Development. I have always been fascinated by technology and how it can solve real-world problems, which is what drew me to this field.

My Journey

My journey into tech started with curiosity — wondering how websites and apps work. Over time, that curiosity turned into passion. Today, I spend my time learning new programming languages, building small projects, and dreaming of the day I will create software that makes a difference in people's lives.

I believe that with dedication and the right mindset, anyone can achieve their goals. My motto is: "Never stop learning, because life never stops teaching."
